About CIT Signups

CIT Signups is a centralized platform designed to help Deep Run High School students and staff coordinate and manage event signups for Center for Information Technology (CIT) activities.

Getting Started

Once logged in, you can:

  • View all upcoming CIT events
  • Sign up for activities that interest you
  • View the calendar of all scheduled events
  • Manage your profile and event participation
